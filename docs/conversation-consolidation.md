# Consolidate duplicate conversations

Open the duplicate in the agent Inbox. Enter the primary conversation ID
in the customer details panel and review both subjects and IDs. Confirm the
consolidation to close the duplicate and free its active assignment capacity.
The primary and duplicate keep their own transcripts and attachments.

Both conversations must belong to the same project and contact. Messenger
conversations with a newer request, a provider-thread binding, a linked ticket,
or an active AI answer are ineligible. Owners and admins can consolidate
threads assigned across the team. An agent can consolidate unassigned threads
and threads assigned to them.

Read the duplicate from its original Inbox entry or the primary's duplicate
links. Its original transcript stays readable. A customer who replies through
the old Messenger URL sends to the primary. The Messenger loads the committed
destination and its separate history. A replay of the same reply returns the
original acknowledgement, including after an undo.

Open the duplicate and choose **Undo consolidation** to resume handling it
separately. A previous open conversation re-enters current assignment and
capacity rules. A future follow-up keeps its scheduled time, and a follow-up
that became due reopens. Replies already accepted in the primary stay there.
Reports count active and historical consolidations separately from response
and resolution times.
